# What holds up

Findings and recommendations that survived independent recomputation from the clean tables, the traceability sample, the consistency check and the adversarial pass. Each can be presented with confidence, subject only to the caveats already in the deliverables. Register ids in brackets.

## Findings

- **FY25 deficit and cash.** −$607K; cash $2.06M = 4.4 months (7.5 in FY21, 5.2 in FY24); FY24 +$507K. Reproduce exactly. The deficit is unaffected by the event reclassification (R15). [M1-02]
- **Government share on the audit basis.** 41, 44, 40, 42, 49, 55, 50, 61, 51, 45, 58% for FY15–FY25; FY22–24 mean 52.5%; line 1e equals the audit in FY22 and FY23. [M1-01]
- **Filed institutional base.** $4.43M from 50 funders TY2020–24; institutional $3.34M (42 funders), DAF $1.09M (25%); GWCF $1,206,500 = 27%; top five 63%; HHI 1,168. Every bar on leadership slide 6 reproduces. [M1-04]
- **Three public streams, one hub.** OVSJG $0.37M (FY20) to $1.05M (FY26); HUD CoC via TCP $0.24M (2019) to $1.77M (FY2025, three projects); DHS via TCP $1.42M in FY2024 (transitional housing $830K, Sisters Rise $593K). The TCP channel carries $1.8–2.2M of FY25 government revenue under every apportionment tested, so "protect the TCP channel first" holds even though the $960K point estimate does not. [M2-01, M2-05 in part]
- **Ceilings vs recognized, as a question.** FY24 $2.62M of $3.60M (73%); FY25 $2.89M of $4.29M (67%). The ratios reproduce; the audited FY22–23 SEFA locates the gap in the CoC stream, which strengthens the "draw-down reconciliation" recommendation. [M2-02]

## Recommendations that stand

- Reconcile award ceilings to recognized revenue, starting with the CoC stream, and confirm the DHS contract values with TCP (recommendation 1, sharpened by R14).
- Protect the TCP channel: defend the three CoC renewals in the FY2026 competition and scope a PSH expansion project (recommendation 1 and 5).
- Multi-year general-operating conversations with GWCF (Health Equity Fund successor), Cafritz (1 March or 1 November) and Fowler (rolling); steward Abell after its 2023 grant (recommendation 2, with the R08 wording).
- Re-engage Many Hands in the 4–15 January 2027 window; World Bank Community Connections Fund and Jane Bancroft Robinson as lapsed funders (recommendation 2).
- Start a named major-gifts and DAF program; peers with Calvary's profile raise 37–51% of filed grants through DAFs against Calvary's 25% (recommendation 2).
- Treat corporate relationships (MGAC and others) as a separate, relationship-led track and quantify cash vs in-kind (recommendation 2).
- A six-month reserve target of about $2.8M and a plan to close the FY25 gap (recommendation 3).
- An advocacy and monitoring calendar for the March–July budget season, now with the FY2026 CoC awards (about December 2026), the EHV end date (31 December 2026) and the one-time nature of the FY27 OVSJG restoration on it (recommendation 5).
- Expansion near first (workforce and behavioral health), health as a partnership, families not suggested; with the staffing caveat from R09 (recommendation 4).
- The verdict's rating: moderate-to-high pressure from channel concentration and liquidity, not from the government share.
