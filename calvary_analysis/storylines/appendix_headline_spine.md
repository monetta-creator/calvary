# Appendix decks: headline spines

Four appendix decks, split by topic. Every slide follows the headline rules and carries the source/method/trace footnote. Tables longer than one slide paginate with a continued headline.

## Appendix A: Calvary and government funding
1. Appendix A: Calvary and Government Funding *(title)*
2. Calvary's revenue series agrees across five sources within 1% in every year, so the trajectory rests on firm ground. (reconciliation table)
3. Government revenue on the audit basis exceeded 990 line 1e by $1.48M in FY21 and matched it in FY22 and FY23. (m1_03 data table)
4. Revenue, expenses, net assets, cash and months of cash by year show the FY22 capital event and the FY23 and FY25 deficits. (m1_01, m1_04, m1_05 tables)
5. Event contributions and net event income by year show the FY25 collapse against positive net income. (m1_06)
6. Fifty funders filed grants to Calvary in TY2020–24 and the top ten supplied 76% of the dollars. (m1_07 full table)
7. OVSJG awards to Calvary rose from $369K to $1.05M between DC FY2020 and FY2026, with the one-time $3.68M building award in FY2022. (m2_01 OVSJG detail)
8. HUD CoC awards to Calvary's three projects rose from $240K (2019) to $1.77M (FY2025) through TCP. (m2_01 CoC detail)
9. DC's FY2024 oversight responses are the only public record of Calvary's DHS contracts: $830K for transitional housing and $593K for Sisters Rise. (m2_01 DHS detail)
10. Calvary's single audits for FY22 and FY23 show $4.07M and $0.94M of federal expenditures, almost all passed through DC agencies. (SEFA table)
11. One-time awards (SLFRF, PPP, HUD CPF, DHCD, LISC) total $4.27M and are excluded from the recurring base. (m2_01b)
12. The award-ceiling alignment uses DC fiscal years directly, HUD competition years lagged one year, and the FY2024 DHS value carried forward. (m2_02 table)
13. Seven streams carry distinct renewal mechanisms and risks. (m2_03 table)
14. DC budget lines for OVSJG, the DHS continuum, PSH and DV services by stage and year. (m2_04 table)
15. Fourteen budget events of $2M or more touched Calvary's lines since 2023. (m2_06 table)
16. Calvary's share of the OVSJG and CoC pools by year. (m2_05 table)
17. The six scenarios and their parameters are reproducible from config/scenarios.yaml. (m2_07 tables)
